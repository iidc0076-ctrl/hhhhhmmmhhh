# USER CONDUCT POLICY

**Effective Date:** June 16, 2025  
**Last Updated:** June 16, 2025

## 1. INTRODUCTION

This User Conduct Policy establishes specific behavioral guidelines and enforcement procedures for users of our Discord-based trading analysis service QuantVision. This policy supplements our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, providing detailed rules to ensure a safe, legal, and professional environment.

## 2. SIGNAL DISTRIBUTION POLICIES

### 2.1 Absolute Prohibition on Signal Sharing
Users are strictly prohibited from:
- **COPYING SIGNALS:** Taking screenshots, recordings, or written copies of any trading signals
- **EXTERNAL DISTRIBUTION:** Sharing signals on social media, websites, forums, or other platforms
- **RESELLING CONTENT:** Monetizing, selling, or licensing any analysis or signals
- **GROUP SHARING:** Distributing signals in private Discord servers, Telegram groups, or other communities
- **AUTOMATED REDISTRIBUTION:** Using bots or automated systems to republish content

### 2.2 Personal Use Only
All signals and analysis are licensed exclusively for:
- Personal trading decisions by the authorized account holder
- Individual educational purposes
- Private research and learning activities
- Non-commercial personal use within the authorized Discord environment

### 2.3 Signal Sharing Detection
We employ advanced monitoring systems to detect unauthorized distribution including:
- Cross-platform content matching algorithms
- Timestamp correlation analysis
- User behavior pattern recognition
- Community reporting mechanisms
- Automated takedown request systems

### 2.4 Enforcement for Signal Violations
Violations result in:
- **First Offense:** Immediate temporary suspension (7-30 days)
- **Second Offense:** Extended suspension (60-90 days) + warning documentation
- **Third Offense:** Permanent account termination + legal action consideration
- **Commercial Distribution:** Immediate permanent ban + pursue damages

## 3. TRADING AND FINANCIAL CONDUCT

### 3.1 Investment Advice Prohibition
Users must not:
- Provide personalized investment recommendations to other users
- Offer financial planning or wealth management advice
- Make specific buy/sell recommendations based on signals
- Guarantee trading outcomes or profitability
- Promote get-rich-quick schemes or unrealistic returns

### 3.2 Market Manipulation Prevention
Strictly prohibited activities include:
- Coordinating group trading actions based on signals
- Using signals for pump-and-dump schemes
- Manipulating market prices through coordinated efforts
- Engaging in wash trading or other deceptive practices
- Creating artificial market demand or supply

### 3.3 Risk Disclosure Requirements
When discussing trading, users must:
- Include appropriate risk warnings in discussions
- Acknowledge that trading involves potential for total loss
- Avoid making unrealistic profit claims
- Encourage others to conduct independent research
- Recommend consulting qualified financial advisors

### 3.4 Regulatory Compliance
Users are responsible for:
- Complying with securities regulations in their jurisdiction
- Understanding licensing requirements for investment advice
- Reporting trading activities as required by tax authorities
- Ensuring legal capacity to engage in trading activities
- Following anti-money laundering (AML) requirements

## 4. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

### 4.1 Professional Communication
All interactions must maintain:
- **RESPECTFUL TONE:** Professional and courteous communication
- **FACTUAL ACCURACY:** No spreading of misinformation or false claims
- **CONSTRUCTIVE DIALOGUE:** Focus on educational and helpful exchanges
- **CULTURAL SENSITIVITY:** Respect for diverse backgrounds and perspectives
- **LANGUAGE APPROPRIATENESS:** Clean language free from profanity or offensive content

### 4.2 Prohibited Communications
Users may not engage in:
- **HARASSMENT:** Targeting individuals with unwanted or aggressive messages
- **DISCRIMINATION:** Content based on race, gender, religion, nationality, or other protected characteristics
- **SPAM:** Repetitive messages, advertisements, or unsolicited promotions
- **THREATS:** Any form of intimidation, threats of violence, or hostile behavior
- **DOXXING:** Sharing personal information of other users without consent

### 4.3 Educational Content Guidelines
When sharing educational content, users must:
- Clearly distinguish between facts and opinions
- Provide sources for factual claims about markets
- Avoid making definitive predictions about future market movements
- Include appropriate disclaimers about trading risks
- Respect intellectual property rights of educational materials

## 5. ACCOUNT SECURITY AND INTEGRITY

### 5.1 Account Protection Requirements
Users must:
- **SECURE CREDENTIALS:** Maintain strong, unique passwords for Discord accounts
- **TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION:** Enable 2FA where available
- **DEVICE SECURITY:** Keep devices secure and updated with latest security patches
- **ACCESS MONITORING:** Regularly review account activity for unauthorized access
- **IMMEDIATE REPORTING:** Report suspected security breaches within 24 hours

### 5.2 Account Sharing Prohibition
Strictly prohibited:
- Sharing Discord account credentials with others
- Allowing family members or friends to use your account
- Using proxy accounts or alternate identities
- Creating multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions
- Transferring account access to third parties

### 5.3 Identity Verification Compliance
During verification processes, users must:
- Provide accurate and truthful information
- Submit genuine identification documents
- Respond promptly to verification requests
- Update information when circumstances change
- Cooperate fully with security review procedures

## 6. TECHNICAL USE POLICIES

### 6.1 API and System Usage
Users are prohibited from:
- **RATE LIMIT ABUSE:** Making excessive requests to overwhelm systems
- **AUTOMATION ABUSE:** Using unauthorized bots or scripts
- **REVERSE ENGINEERING:** Attempting to extract or copy proprietary algorithms
- **SYSTEM EXPLOITATION:** Taking advantage of bugs or vulnerabilities
- **RESOURCE ABUSE:** Actions that degrade service performance for other users

### 6.2 Data Mining and Scraping
Forbidden activities include:
- Systematic downloading or copying of service data
- Using automated tools to extract signals or analysis
- Building databases of historical signals or market data
- Creating competing services using extracted data
- Selling or licensing scraped information

### 6.3 Security Testing Restrictions
Users may not:
- Conduct unauthorized penetration testing
- Attempt to bypass security measures
- Test for vulnerabilities without explicit permission
- Share security issues publicly before responsible disclosure
- Use discovered vulnerabilities for unauthorized access

## 7. CONTENT AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 7.1 Respect for Proprietary Content
Users must respect:
- **ALGORITHMS:** Proprietary trading algorithms and methodologies
- **ANALYSIS CONTENT:** Original market analysis and commentary
- **EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS:** Training content and documentation
- **BRANDING:** Trademarks, logos, and service names
- **DATA COMPILATIONS:** Processed and aggregated market information

### 7.2 User-Generated Content Standards
When creating content, users must ensure:
- Original creation or proper attribution of sources
- Compliance with copyright and intellectual property laws
- Accuracy of factual claims and data
- Appropriate disclaimers for opinions and predictions
- Respect for the rights of other content creators

### 7.3 Fair Use Guidelines
Limited use of service content is permitted for:
- Personal educational purposes
- Academic research (with proper citation)
- Critical commentary or review
- News reporting about the service
- Parody or transformative use (within legal bounds)

## 8. COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

### 8.1 Positive Community Environment
Users should foster:
- **COLLABORATIVE LEARNING:** Sharing knowledge and helping others learn
- **CONSTRUCTIVE FEEDBACK:** Providing helpful suggestions and improvements
- **INCLUSIVE PARTICIPATION:** Welcoming users from diverse backgrounds
- **PROBLEM-SOLVING FOCUS:** Working together to address challenges
- **MUTUAL RESPECT:** Treating all community members with dignity

### 8.2 Conflict Resolution
When disagreements arise:
- Address issues directly but respectfully with involved parties
- Use private messages for personal disputes
- Involve moderators when resolution cannot be reached
- Focus on factual disagreements rather than personal attacks
- Accept moderation decisions gracefully

### 8.3 Reporting Mechanisms
Users should report:
- **POLICY VIOLATIONS:** Any behavior that violates community standards
- **TECHNICAL ISSUES:** Bugs, errors, or system problems
- **SECURITY CONCERNS:** Suspected unauthorized access or data breaches
- **CONTENT VIOLATIONS:** Copyright infringement or inappropriate material
- **HARASSMENT:** Any form of bullying, threats, or discriminatory behavior

## 9. ENFORCEMENT PROCEDURES

### 9.1 Violation Assessment
When evaluating violations, we consider:
- **SEVERITY:** Impact and potential harm of the violation
- **INTENT:** Whether violation was deliberate or accidental
- **FREQUENCY:** Pattern of behavior or isolated incident
- **COOPERATION:** User response to correction attempts
- **HISTORY:** Previous violations and enforcement actions

### 9.2 Progressive Enforcement
Standard enforcement progression:
1. **INFORMAL WARNING:** Private message addressing minor violations
2. **FORMAL WARNING:** Official documented warning for repeated issues
3. **TEMPORARY SUSPENSION:** Short-term access restriction (1-30 days)
4. **EXTENDED SUSPENSION:** Longer restriction for serious violations (30-90 days)
5. **PERMANENT TERMINATION:** Permanent account closure for severe or repeated violations

### 9.3 Immediate Termination Offenses
Certain violations result in immediate permanent termination:
- Commercial distribution of signals or content
- Serious security breaches or hacking attempts
- Threats of violence or doxxing other users
- Multiple account creation to circumvent restrictions
- Legal violations including securities fraud or market manipulation

### 9.4 Appeal Process
Users may appeal enforcement actions by:
- Submitting formal appeal within 30 days of action
- Providing detailed explanation and supporting evidence
- Demonstrating understanding of violated policies
- Committing to compliance with all future policies
- Accepting additional monitoring or restrictions if reinstated

## 10. LEGAL COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

### 10.1 Securities Law Compliance
Users must understand and comply with:
- Investment advisor registration requirements in their jurisdiction
- Disclosure obligations for material conflicts of interest
- Prohibitions on insider trading and market manipulation
- Record-keeping requirements for investment-related communications
- Client suitability and know-your-customer obligations

### 10.2 Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
Users are responsible for:
- Verifying the source of funds used in trading activities
- Reporting suspicious transactions as required by law
- Maintaining records of large financial transactions
- Complying with customer identification programs
- Understanding sanctions and prohibited persons lists

### 10.3 Tax Compliance
Users must:
- Report trading gains and losses as required by tax authorities
- Maintain adequate records for tax reporting purposes
- Understand the tax implications of different trading strategies
- Comply with withholding and reporting requirements
- Seek professional tax advice for complex situations

## 11. INTERNATIONAL USERS

### 11.1 Local Law Compliance
International users must:
- Research and comply with local financial regulations
- Understand restrictions on foreign financial services
- Ensure legal capacity to engage with US-based services
- Comply with currency exchange and reporting requirements
- Understand tax implications of international trading

### 11.2 Cultural Sensitivity
When interacting in the international community:
- Respect different time zones and business customs
- Use clear, simple English for non-native speakers
- Avoid cultural references that may not translate
- Be patient with language barriers
- Respect different regulatory environments and trading practices

## 12. MONITORING AND COMPLIANCE

### 12.1 Automated Monitoring Systems
We employ sophisticated systems to monitor:
- Signal distribution across multiple platforms
- Unusual trading patterns or coordinated activities
- Communication patterns that may indicate violations
- Technical system abuse or unauthorized access attempts
- Compliance with rate limits and usage restrictions

### 12.2 Human Review Procedures
Human moderators review:
- Complex policy violations requiring judgment
- Appeals and disputed enforcement actions
- Reports of serious misconduct or illegal activity
- Patterns of behavior that may indicate systematic violations
- Requests for policy clarification or guidance

### 12.3 Continuous Improvement
We regularly:
- Review and update policies based on community feedback
- Analyze enforcement effectiveness and fairness
- Train staff on evolving regulations and best practices
- Engage with legal experts to ensure compliance
- Communicate policy changes clearly to users

## 13. CONTACT AND SUPPORT

### 13.1 Policy Questions
For questions about this User Conduct Policy:
- Contact support through official Discord channels
- Reference specific policy sections in your inquiry
- Provide context for your situation or question
- Allow 48-72 hours for detailed policy responses

### 13.2 Violation Reporting
To report policy violations:
- Use the dedicated reporting system within Discord
- Provide specific details and evidence when possible
- Include relevant usernames, timestamps, and screenshots
- Follow up if no response is received within 5 business days

### 13.3 Emergency Situations
For urgent security or safety concerns:
- Contact administrators immediately through priority channels
- Provide clear description of the immediate threat or risk
- Follow up with detailed written report within 24 hours
- Cooperate fully with any investigation procedures
