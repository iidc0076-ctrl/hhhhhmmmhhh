## 1. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, processing, and protection of personal information by our Discord-based trading analysis service QuantVision ("Service," "Bot," "We," "Us," or "Our"). By using our Service, you ("User," "You," or "Your") acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy.

### 1.1 Service Description
QuantVision provides automated trading analysis, technical indicators, and educational content through Discord bot commands. We do not provide investment advice, recommendations, or financial planning services.

### 1.2 Jurisdictional Compliance
This Privacy Policy complies with applicable data protection laws including but not limited to:
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Automatically Collected Information
When you interact with our Service, we automatically collect:
- **Discord User Identifier:** Your unique Discord user ID for service authentication
- **Server Information:** Discord server (guild) IDs where the bot is used
- **Command Usage Data:** Commands executed, timestamps, and frequency of use
- **Technical Data:** IP addresses (through Discord's infrastructure), device information, and connection metadata
- **Error Logs:** System errors and debugging information (containing no personal identifiers)

### 2.2 User-Provided Information
We collect information you voluntarily provide:
- **Verification Data:** Information submitted during account verification processes
- **Preferences:** Trading preferences, notification settings, and customization choices
- **Feedback:** Bug reports, feature requests, and user communications

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
We explicitly do not collect:
- Real names, addresses, or phone numbers
- Financial account information or trading account credentials
- Personal trading positions or portfolio data
- Private messages not directly related to bot commands
- Sensitive personal information including biometric or health data
